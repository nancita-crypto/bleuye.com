Frequently asked
questions

How long does a Bleuye box stay cold?+

Our first prototype held 8 °C or below for 41 hours in a 26–28 °C room. On mild ground lanes that models to about 4 days. Lab qualification is next.

Which carriers do you use?+

Your usual ones. Bleuye boxes ship UPS or FedEx Ground. We choose ground when the lane allows and flag when it doesn’t.

Do patients need an app?+

No. They get texts in your pharmacy’s name and can scan or tap the box to see that it stayed cold.

What happens to the box after delivery?+

Patients drop it at a partner collector nearby. We pick up monthly and remake the panels.

Where can I start?+

One lane, in our pilot city. Tell us the route you ship most and we’ll check it for ground.
